# Indian state startup missions, national schemes and public AI compute for a solo, unincorporated software/AI founder (as of 12 November 2026)

Research date: 11-12 November 2026. Several official portals could not be read by automated tools (see the access log at the end of each section). Labels used: **[OFFICIAL]** = government or programme-owner page read directly; **[OFFICIAL-ARCHIVE]** = official page read via the Internet Archive; **[SECONDARY]** = news, aggregator or consultant site; **[SEARCH-SUMMARY]** = only seen as a search-engine summary, not opened. Aggregators (startupgrantsindia.com, incorpx.io, fundsforngos, startupflora, techdodo, productgrowth.in) are commercial sites and are not affiliated with government; startupgrantsindia.com says this itself in its footer.

Status rule applied: a call is only described as "open" if a dated source shows a deadline after 12 November 2026. Everything else is "closed", "rolling (unconfirmed)" or "status unknown".

## Q1. State programmes: Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala (eligibility for individuals vs registered entities, benefits, open calls, deadlines)

### Takeaway
Most state money (grants) needs a registered entity, and usually one registered in that state. The main routes that take an individual before incorporation are challenges and pre-incubation: Telangana's TISC 2026 (closes 20 Nov 2026, individuals allowed, software/AI allowed, per a secondary source), Kerala's Idea Grant (you can apply as an individual, but you must incorporate and get a KSUM Unique ID before money is paid; closes 31 Dec 2026 per secondary sources), and RTIH "Future Founders" in AP (for AP residents). Karnataka ELEVATE requires a Karnataka-registered startup with at least two founders, so a solo founder does not qualify.

### Inferences
- For a founder with no company and not resident in Kerala, AP or Karnataka, the only state-level option confirmed open today that takes individuals is TISC (Telangana, closes 20 Nov 2026). It is a pilot/procurement challenge, not a grant.
- If the founder lives in Kerala, the Idea Grant can be applied for now as an individual, with incorporation delayed until the grant is approved. This is the cheapest "grant first, incorporate later" route found.
- Karnataka ELEVATE's two-founder minimum excludes a solo founder even after incorporation, unless a co-founder joins.
- Because the APIS and RTIH domains named in the brief are parked, any "apply" link on them should not be trusted. Use apis.ap.gov.in or apit.ap.gov.in links only.

- WE Hub's current programmes and TSIC's 2026 calls were not confirmed from official pages.
- The official TISC 2026 page was not located; dates come from an aggregator.
- apis.ap.gov.in and Startup Karnataka's portal were not read (one parked or unknown, one DNS failure).
- No official KSUM notice for the Idea Grant's 31 Dec 2026 deadline was found. The grants portal is behind a login.

## Q2. National programmes: MeitY (MSH, TIDE 2.0, GENESIS, SAMRIDH), Startup India Seed Fund, DST NIDHI (PRAYAS, EIR, iTBI), Atal Innovation Mission (AIC, ANIC)

### Takeaway
Almost nothing at national level is open today for an unincorporated solo founder. GENESIS EIR Cohort 3 (which explicitly allowed individual innovators and AI/ML, up to ₹10 lakh) **closed 3 Sep 2026** and was limited to Tier-II/III cities. NIDHI-PRAYAS and NIDHI-EIR do accept individuals, but they run through host incubators with their own calls; none confirmed open. TIDE 2.0 runs through partner incubators with no national call. SISFS needs a DPIIT-recognised entity (at most 2 years old) applying through an incubator, and its new-application status for 2026 is disputed. ANIC targets prototype-stage startups and MSMEs solving listed sector challenges; no open call.

### Inferences
- GENESIS EIR is the national scheme that best fits a solo, unincorporated AI founder (individual allowed, AI/ML named, ₹10 lakh). But Cohort 3 is closed, and it needs a Tier-II/III city plus physical incubation. Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai and similar cities are generally Tier-I, so a founder living there would likely not qualify (this is inference; the guideline excerpt does not list cities). Watch for Cohort 4.
- NIDHI-PRAYAS accepts individuals and is not formally limited to hardware, but it is designed around building prototypes. Whether a pure-software AI project qualifies depends on each PRAYAS centre's call. Do not assume eligibility; check each centre's call text.
- ANIC's challenge areas are sector problems and are largely hardware or deployment oriented. A general-purpose software AI product would need to map onto a specific listed challenge.
- SISFS, TIDE 2.0 startup grants and SAMRIDH in practice all need an incorporated, DPIIT-recognised startup.

### Gaps
- Official MSH, SISFS, AIM and NIDHI pages could not be read, so their current open or closed status is unverified.
- No official list of which cities count as "Tier-II/III" for GENESIS was obtained.
- No 2026 NIDHI-EIR call was found; the PRAYAS software-eligibility rule was not confirmed from an official DST document.
- TIDE 2.0 amounts and eligibility at i-TIC and SINE were not read.

## Q3. IndiaAI Mission: compute portal eligibility, subsidy, pricing, commitments, documents; IndiaAI Startups Global; Fellowships; AIKosh

### Takeaway
The IndiaAI compute portal has **no plain "individual" category**. Its user categories are Researchers & Academia, Startups & MSMEs, IndiaAI Fellowship holders, Students, Early-stage Startups, Early-stage Researchers, and Government entities. A solo founder without a company would need to qualify as a student, a researcher (h-index ≥ 5, a publication in a journal with impact factor > 2.5, or 150+ citations), an early-stage researcher, or an IndiaAI Fellow. The "Early-stage Startup" track needs 4 years of AI/ML experience plus a letter from the IndiaAI CEO or an officer of Joint Secretary rank or above. Published allocations show the subsidy at up to about 40% of the bill. Prices run from about ₹45/hr (L4) to ₹1,224/hr (8×H100), with a headline average of about ₹65/GPU-hr. Requests under 5,000 GPU-hours are auto-approved. The IndiaAI Fellowship 2026-27 (students only) closes 30 Nov 2026. IndiaAI Startups Global cohort 2 closed 28 Jun 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Compute portal: eligibility (official page, archived 24 Apr 2026)** **[OFFICIAL-ARCHIVE]** — [compute.indiaai.gov.in/eligibilitycriteria (Wayback 20260424)](https://web.archive.org/web/20260424111207/https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/eligibilitycriteria)
- The live portal refused or timed out connections from our tools on 11-12 Nov 2026 (ECONNREFUSED or timeout), so the archive copy was used.

**Compute portal: process, pricing, rules** (secondary summary of the portal FAQ and Ready Reckoner) **[SECONDARY]** — [productgrowth.in (checked 1 Nov 2026)](https://productgrowth.in/insights/ai-ml/indiaai-compute-portal-startup-gpu-access/)

## Q5. Registration facts: DPIIT entity types; OPC vs Pvt Ltd vs LLP costs and compliance; what is possible without incorporating

### Takeaway
DPIIT recognition (the gate for SISFS, compute-portal startup tracks, most TIDE and state grants) is only for a Private Limited Company, Partnership Firm, LLP or Cooperative Society. It is **not available to an individual or sole proprietor**. The official page now allows up to 10 years from incorporation (20 for DeepTech) and turnover below ₹200 crore (₹300 crore for DeepTech). Tax exemptions (80-IAC) apply only to Pvt Ltd or LLP. An OPC is legally a private company, and secondary sources treat it as eligible (KSUM explicitly lists OPC). MCA government fees for small companies are low (reported nil up to ₹15 lakh authorised capital, but sources disagree), with state stamp duty extra. Official MCA fee pages blocked automated access.

### Cited Findings
- **DPIIT recognition page** **[OFFICIAL]**, read 12 Nov 2026 — [Startup India: Recognition](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startupgov/startup_recognition_page.html):
  - "The startup should be incorporated as a Private Limited Company or registered as a Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership, or Cooperative Society."
  - "Turnover should be less than INR 200 Crores (INR 300 Crores for DeepTech startups) in any of the previous financial years."
  - "An entity shall be considered a startup for up to 10 years (20 years for DeepTech startups) from the date of its incorporation."
  - It must work on innovation or improvement and have employment or wealth potential. Entities formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business are excluded.
  - Recognition is applied for on the National Single Window System (nsws.gov.in), form "Registration as a Startup". After recognition, a startup can apply for 80-IAC and Section 56 exemptions. "Only Private limited or a Limited Liability Partnership is eligible for Tax exemption."
- The IndiaAI compute portal's own DPIIT explainer still says ₹100 crore turnover and 10 years, and omits cooperatives. This is older than the DPIIT page. **[OFFICIAL-ARCHIVE]** — [compute portal eligibility (archive)](https://web.archive.org/web/20260424111207/https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/eligibilitycriteria)
- KSUM Idea Grant disbursement requires "company incorporation (PVT/LLP/OPC)" plus a KSUM Unique ID. **[SECONDARY]** — [startupflora: KSUM Idea Grant](https://startupflora.com/blog/ksum-idea-grant-2026-amount-eligibility-and-how-to-apply-online)
- MCA fees (all **[SECONDARY]**, conflicting):
  - SPICe+ government filing fee is nil for authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh — [calcguru: OPC 2026](https://calcguru.in/?p=4318)
  - vs "zero filing fee up to ₹1 lakh" — [incorpx: company registration cost](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/company-registration-cost-india)
  - Name reservation (SPICe+ Part A) about ₹1,000; the name is held for 20 days — [incorpx: SPICe+ 2026](https://www.incorpx.io/blog/spice-plus-form-company-incorporation-guide-2026)
  - Stamp duty on the MoA/AoA varies by state — [registerkaro: OPC fees](https://www.registerkaro.in/post/opc-registration-fees)
  - All-in OPC cost with professional help is estimated at ₹6,000-₹23,000 — [kanakkupillai](https://www.kanakkupillai.com/learn/what-is-the-one-person-company-registration-fees-in-india/); [calcguru](https://calcguru.in/?p=4318)

**What an individual can do without incorporating (from the sources above):**
- Apply for TISC 2026 (Telangana; individuals; closes 20 Nov 2026) — [startupgrantsindia: TISC](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/tisc-telangana-innovation-startup-challenge-2026)

- IndiaAI compute only as a student, researcher, early-stage researcher or IndiaAI Fellow; IndiaAI Fellowship if a full-time student (closes 30 Nov 2026) — [compute eligibility (archive)](https://web.archive.org/web/20260424111207/https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/eligibilitycriteria); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/indiaai-fellowship-programme-opens-for-ay-2026-27-check-eligibility-funding-deadline-562251-2026-10-17)

### Inferences
- OPC is not named on the DPIIT page, but an OPC is a private company under the Companies Act. DPIIT recognition of OPCs is common in practice, but this was not verified from an official DPIIT statement. For a solo founder, OPC is the only company form that needs no second person (Pvt Ltd needs at least 2 directors and shareholders; LLP needs at least 2 partners). Note, however, that Karnataka ELEVATE needs 2 founders regardless.
- DPIIT and 80-IAC benefits favour Pvt Ltd or LLP. An OPC can be converted to a Pvt Ltd later when a second shareholder joins.
- Many "individual-eligible" grants (KSUM, GENESIS, PRAYAS) still require incorporation before or soon after the money is released. A sensible order is: apply as an individual → incorporate on selection → DPIIT on NSWS.

### Gaps
- Official MCA fee schedule (SPICe+, AOC-4, MGT-7, LLP Form 8/Form 11) and state stamp-duty tables for Telangana, AP, Karnataka and Kerala were not read; the secondary sources conflict on the nil-fee threshold (₹15 lakh vs ₹1 lakh).
- Annual compliance costs (auditor fees, mandatory audit for OPC/Pvt Ltd vs threshold-based audit for LLP) were not sourced in this pass.
- No official DPIIT statement on OPC eligibility was found.
